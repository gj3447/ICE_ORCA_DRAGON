## Why discovery is hard here

1. **The present results are local.**  A fixed parameter box, one harmonic
   packet, or one cutoff identity cannot establish a global operator, algebra,
   state, or observable.
2. **Gauge consistency is conjunctive.**  One correct DH packet does not imply
   DD, HH, Jacobi, BFV nilpotency, or anomaly freedom.
3. **Quantization adds choices.**  Extension data, ordering, domains, test
   spaces and measures are not fixed by a classical identity.  The selected-H
   RAQ construction cannot be silently reused as raw-C RAQ.
4. **A physical prediction needs a state and an observable.**  Until the
   physical product, clock, state, reheating transfer and normalization are
   fixed, a likelihood would compare an underdefined quantity.
5. **Computational novelty is not yet scientific novelty.**  A repository-local
   exact identity becomes a discovery only after its scope is shown to be
   nontrivial relative to established theory, robust to conventions and
   independent checks, and capable of changing a theoretical or empirical
   conclusion.

## Promotion rule
