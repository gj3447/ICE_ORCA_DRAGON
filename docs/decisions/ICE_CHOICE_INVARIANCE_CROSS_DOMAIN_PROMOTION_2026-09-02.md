# Choice-invariance × cross-domain promotion boundary

Date: 2026-09-02

Status: **ACTIVE repository-wide promotion boundary**

Scope: every ICE ORCA DRAGON research graph when a scoped calculation is proposed for broader
model-level, physical, new-physics, empirical, or TOE interpretation

This is a review boundary, not a research contract, execution permit, scientific result, or demand that
every small calculation already possess an observable.

## Canonical question

> 이 효과는 어떤 선택을 바꿔도 남으며, 다른 관측 영역에서도 같은 이유로 나타나는가?

Operational English:

> Does the effect persist under the predeclared admissible changes of choice relevant to its mechanism,
> and does that same mechanism reach at least two independently defined downstream consumers within
> the same research graph?

The qualification is essential. “Every choice” does not include arbitrary changes of theory, physical
boundary condition, or question. It means the admissible family that should describe the same physical
content: for example basis, chart, gauge, regulator refinement, equivalent polarization, endpoint
normalization, or a declared nuisance convention. If two settings represent physically distinct states
or self-adjoint extensions, dependence may be real; the graph must record that distinction instead of
calling it a failure of invariance.

“Another observational domain” does not mean another ontology graph. Hypercomplex, legacy, CPT, and
IG-RUEQFT keep independent evidence boundaries. It means a second independently defined observable,
constraint, or downstream consumer in the same declared programme. Reusing the same data, residual,
fit, or transformed variable is not independence.

## Promotion test

A result may remain a valid scoped computational fact without passing this test. Before widening its
scope or calling it a model/physics/new-physics candidate, human review must be able to name:

1. **Selection family** — the predeclared basis, chart, gauge, contour, regulator, extension, clock,
   state, normalization, reheating, prior, or data-cut choices that are scientifically admissible.
2. **Mechanism-carrying typed object** — the exact object supposed to persist: an integer intersection
   vector, cohomology class, index, spectral-measure class, positive rigging form, holonomy, normalized
   relation, or another explicitly defined invariant.
3. **Invariance and null criterion** — what equality/equivalence must survive, what variation is allowed,
   and what observation kills or narrows the interpretation. A stable zero or no-go is a valid result.
4. **Independent checks** — at least two scientifically meaningful formulations, implementations,
   regulator levels, or analytic/numerical routes where such a comparison exists. A tolerance sweep of
   one backend is not independence.
5. **Two in-graph consumers** — two independently defined downstream constraints or observables that
   use the same typed object for the same mechanism. Topical similarity and equal-looking numbers do
   not qualify.
6. **Dominant false-signal control** — the most plausible basis, projection, gauge, discretization,
   extension, look-elsewhere, provenance, state, or normalization artifact and its explicit discriminator.

If any item is absent, the result stays within its declared scope or remains supporting/inconclusive. The
rule does not invent an evidence edge, authorize a successor calculation, or turn a missing consumer
into a blocker. `BLOCKED_BY` is added only when the downstream question literally cannot be evaluated
without the upstream object.

This is a theory-mechanism promotion rule, not a universal definition of discovery. A single directly
observed anomaly may still be reported at its measured scope when independently replicated and globally
calibrated. Without the cross-domain link it remains an empirical anomaly; the common new-physics
mechanism is not yet established. The rule must never be used to hide a null result or real measurement.

## Contemplative verdict on the current repository

No current ICE result passes both halves of the canonical question at model/physics scope.

- Hypercomplex work contains finite algebraic invariants, but no demonstrated physics consumer.
- Legacy numerical matches fail the stored signal/provenance gates rather than recur independently.
- IG-RUEQFT supplies a useful scoped negative result, but not an interacting continuum discriminator.
- CPT P1 has a narrow normalized real-root theorem, but no nonreal spectral object or independent physical
  consumer. P4 has the correct conceptual chain but not its first actual endpoint theorem. P2–P6 provide
  calibration and open consistency targets, not a completed invariant-to-observable route. P7 is not ready
  for likelihood.

The strongest present *research intuition*, not evidence, is that a source-derived G1 relative cycle might
fix a stable global integer vector which is then consumed by the G2/G3 state construction and survives into
two normalized observables. The equally valuable negative outcome is a complete stable zero vector or a
proof that the object depends on an inadmissible choice. Either removes a branch without manufacturing a
signal.

The next deep opportunity is therefore not another isolated numerical coincidence. It is one of:

1. a complete G1 global invariant or no-go;
2. a non-removable P2–P5 cohomology/anomaly class rather than a projected residual;
3. an actual P4 endpoint→m→measure→positive-RAQ equivalence or obstruction;
4. only downstream of those, one dimensionless cross-sector discriminator.

## Graph encoding rule

Do not mint a generic “choice invariant” claim before the tests exist. For an actual result, reuse the
native relation vocabulary:

```text
claim --VALID_WITHIN→ scope(selection family)
claim --HAS_EVIDENCE/(SUPPORTS|CONTRADICTS)→ evidence
evidence --RECORDED_IN→ artifact
claim --MOTIVATES→ downstream open_problem
downstream open_problem --BLOCKED_BY→ prerequisite   # only when literally necessary
```

The repository-wide policy is stored once and exposed through a navigation-only collection path. It is not
cloned into four graph-local pseudo-claims, and no cross-graph evidence edge is created. Native ontology
JSON remains authoritative; JSON-LD/RDF, SHACL, SPARQL, GraphRAG, MCP and RO-Crate remain derived query,
validation, retrieval or interchange layers.
