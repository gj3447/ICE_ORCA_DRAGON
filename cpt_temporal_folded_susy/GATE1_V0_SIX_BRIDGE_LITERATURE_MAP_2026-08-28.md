## Result first

The literature supports a technically meaningful route from the selected homogeneous V0 workbench to a
better constrained quantum-cosmology model, but it does **not** supply one end-to-end theorem. The six
bridges belong to different theorem classes and should not be treated as one serial calculation.

1. A positive classical rescaling (H=fC) need not preserve the quantum theory. Raw-(C) ordering,
   self-adjoint domain, test space, rigging map and observable algebra must be compared explicitly.
2. For the already selected multiplication realization
   (h(\kappa,p)=3p^2-2\hbar^2\kappa^2), (p=0) is not a new finite endpoint of the auxiliary
   self-adjoint operator. The natural full zero-fiber completion is the direct sum of the (p>0) and
   (p<0) branches; an origin atom or branch gluing is extra structure.
3. Fourier-integral-operator theory gives a microlocal/asymptotic endpoint transform. An exact closed-operator
   unitary requires matching spectral measure class, multiplicity, domains, boundary forms and observables.
   In particular, the selected (H\to M_c) target cannot be globally exact while also preserving (p),
   because its joint support is (c<3p^2), whereas the declared (M_c,p) target allows all
   (c\in\mathbb R). The viable exact target is raw (C), not the already selected (H).
4. A relative ghost determinant is not an absolute BFV measure. Endpoint polarization, lapse modulus,
   contour, determinant/Pfaffian line orientation, gauge admissibility, boundary Ward identities and gluing
   remain independent data.
5. The classical inhomogeneous test can begin independently once the full action and the closed-(S^3)
   harmonic ledger are declared. Quantum BFV nilpotency remains downstream of ordering, domain and measure
   choices. A finite (S^3) harmonic set is not closed under products, so projection/aliasing remainder must
   not be mislabeled as an anomaly.
6. Relational observables, semiclassical recovery and data comparison are three separate gates. The present
   (V=0), massless-scalar, closed-FRW sector has no standard inflation/reheating mechanism and therefore
   does not by itself produce a CMB likelihood input.

## Repository follow-through on the map

Three independent, unnumbered bounded calculations now sharpen Bridges 3--5.
They are repository workbench evidence, not facts supplied by the papers in
this literature inventory.

- **Bridge 3, scoped progress:** the selected-\(H\) spectral-coordinate map is
  exactly unitary onto the proper target \(p>0,c<3p^2\) and intertwines both
  \(M_h\leftrightarrow M_c\) and \(p\leftrightarrow p\). The explicit target
  witness \(1_{1<p<2,4p^2<c<5p^2}\) has norm squared \(7/3\), while its source
  joint-spectral projection is zero. Hence the previously declared
  unrestricted \((M_c,p)\) target cannot be reached by a \(p\)-preserving exact
  unitary. This is not the old \(W(c,P,p)\) FIO and does not solve the raw-\(C\)
  endpoint problem.
- **Bridge 4, finite relative progress:** for the pinned zero and \(m=2\) odd
  blocks, \(\operatorname{Pf}(A_0\oplus A_1)=-\lambda(\lambda^2+\pi^2)\).
  Its relative sign is fixed on \([1/2,2]\), but the line degenerates at
  \(\lambda=0\). No bosonic contour, endpoint polarization, Gribov census,
  gluing law, continuum line or absolute measure is selected.
- **Bridge 5, kinematic baseline only:** the unit-\(S^3\), one-pole zonal
  \(L=2\) ledger finds explicit discarded \(Z_3,Z_4\) components. Its projected
  associator is exactly reconstructed from those discarded remainders. This
  separates one finite-cutoff artifact from a future anomaly test, but it does
  not construct ADM+matter constraints or test HDA/Jacobi closure.

Accordingly, Bridge 6 remains blocked by an actual classical closure result,
a selected common raw operator domain and absolute BFV boundary/measure data.
Relational/BO/decoherence and likelihood work remains downstream, and the
current \(V=0\) sector still needs an explicit \(V\ne0\) or alternative
perturbation-generation and reheating model before CMB comparison is defined.

This map classifies papers as follows:

- **D — direct:** its theorem or construction is close to the exact bridge question.
- **M — method:** supplies a required mathematical or computational method.
- **B — boundary:** shows why a tempting shortcut is invalid or non-global.
- **O — observational downstream:** usable only after a primordial observable has been derived.

The inventory is broad and primary-source led, but it is not a bibliometric claim that every paper ever
written on these subjects has been found.
